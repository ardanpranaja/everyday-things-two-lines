Two lines explaining a toaster to a 10-year-old — my entry for the make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run".

## The entry

> A toaster is a tiny sunbed for bread: two slices lie back under glowing wires to catch a tan, then pop up once they're golden brown.
> The sunbed picture works because both brown something with steady heat until a timer calls time, but it leaves out that bread can't choose its tan and the glow comes from electric wires, not the sun.

`main.ts` writes those two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader container), after checking them against the competition's gate: exactly two lines, one sentence each, 1–400 characters total.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program exits with an error instead of writing anything if the entry ever stops being exactly two single-sentence lines inside the 1–400 character gate.
